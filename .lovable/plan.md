# Student Pre-Reading: Credix Ethics Lab

A separate reading for students, not part of the app. It takes about 10 minutes and fits the reading part of the 60-minute session. It goes in your Files as a PDF you can print or share, plus an editable Word copy.

## What's in it (about 3 pages, plain language)

1. **Before you start**: what the session is for and how it runs (10 min reading, 5 min video, 45 min case study).
2. **The case in brief**: Credix, Emily Carter, the loan denial and the message she got. No spoilers about the evidence.
3. **Key terms**, each with a one-line definition and a loan example:
   - Transparency vs. explainability (side by side)
   - Black box / opacity, and how opacity can happen at different levels (data, model, how the decision is shown, how the organisation handles it)
   - Risk score and approval cutoff
   - Decision factors / feature importance (a short mention of SHAP and LIME as tools that show which factors mattered)
   - Debt-to-income ratio, credit score and credit checks (the terms Emily's case uses)
   - Human review, appeal and contestability
   - Transparency by design
4. **The AI lifecycle**: Data, Model, Deployment and Monitoring stages, written exactly as the activity describes them.
5. **What makes a good explanation**: a short checklist (why, main factors, what you can do next, how to challenge the decision, not too much detail).
6. **Who cares about the explanation**: the user, the product manager and the compliance reviewer, matching the activity's stakeholders.
7. **Your task in the lab**: a short overview of the 5 steps and the 45-minute timer. No answers.
8. **Quick self-check**: 3 or 4 questions to think about, with no answer key.

## Style
Light theme with white and purple headings in DM Sans, to match the activity. Credix is spelled the same way everywhere. Emily's message uses the activity's exact wording.
